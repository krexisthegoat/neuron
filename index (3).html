Neuromyelitis optica (NMO): the immune system damages myelin in the optic nerve and spinal cord

Healthy neuron

Myelin insulates the axon, so the action potential jumps from node to node (saltatory conduction). The message reaches the axon terminals fast and they release neurotransmitters.

1. Immune system attacks

Antibodies (AQP4-IgG) attack support cells in the optic nerve and spinal cord. Inflammation strips off the myelin sheath.

2. Error in communication

Without myelin, the signal can't jump between nodes. Current leaks out, so it slows down or stops before reaching the axon terminals.

3. Fan's symptoms

Optic nerve: blurry vision, vision loss. Spinal cord: arm numbness & spasms, losing bladder control. Steroids calm inflammation = fewer spasms.
